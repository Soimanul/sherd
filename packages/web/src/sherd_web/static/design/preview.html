Colour

Restrained: charcoal surfaces and bone text carry the page; terracotta marks the one thing to do next. Semantic colours always come with an icon and a word. Green is kept for the privacy counter.

Chart palette

Eight categorical slots in a fixed order, checked for colour-vision separation: adjacent ΔE ≥ 11 under simulated protanopia and deuteranopia, ≥ 20 for normal vision. Scatter plots and small multiples use the first three only; a ninth series becomes “Other”.

Sequential and diverging

One hue from clay to sand, darker means less. Every step clears 3:1 on the chart background, so a heatmap cell never disappears.

Space, shape and motion

A 4 px grid. Small radii on controls, larger on panels. Shadows are short and warm. Motion is fast and only marks a change of state; with reduced motion on, things fade but do not move.

-- Who did I message most in 2025?
SELECT c.display_name, count(*) AS messages
FROM messages m
JOIN contacts c ON c.id = m.contact_id
WHERE m.ts >= '2025-01-01' AND m.ts < '2026-01-01'
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY messages DESC
LIMIT 5;
